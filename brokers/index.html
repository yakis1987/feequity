The four costs worth checking

The cash sweep. Where your uninvested money sits, chosen for you when the account opened. Brokers offer several options paying very different rates, and the default is rarely the best one available to you.

Currency conversion. Applied every time you buy or sell something listed in another currency, built into the rate rather than shown as a line item. The spread between the cheapest and the typical is wider than any commission difference.

Margin interest. Irrelevant if you never borrow, and the largest cost you pay if you do.

Account charges. Mostly gone at the large US brokers, and far less consistent elsewhere.
