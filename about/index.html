Who writes this

My name is Jacob Shasha. I am a private investor who got tired of not knowing what my own account was costing me, worked it out in a spreadsheet, and then found that almost nobody publishes that number in a form you can check.

I am not a licensed financial adviser, a fund manager, an analyst or an accountant. I have no professional qualification in finance and I am not going to pretend otherwise, because a site built on checkable claims cannot start with an unchecked one about its own author.

I have been investing for about fifteen years. Most of what I hold is in US markets, across index funds, ETFs and individual stocks, and some of it in European and Asian markets. That last part is where the interest in this site started, because buying outside your own currency is where the costs stop being obvious.

None of that makes me right about anything. It means I have been paying these fees long enough to want to know exactly what they add up to.

What that means for you

What I can do: read a published fee schedule, open an account and record what actually happens, run the same test across several tools, and report the result with a date and a source. None of that requires a licence. It requires patience and a willingness to publish the inconvenient result.

What I cannot do: tell you what to buy, assess whether something suits your circumstances, or take responsibility for a decision you make. Nothing on this site is personal advice, and no page here knows anything about your situation.

Why it exists

Every broker and every free tracker will happily show you what your portfolio is worth. Almost none of them will show you what it costs to hold, because that number is not in their interest. It is a real number, it compounds, and it is knowable. This site works it out.

How to check me

Three pages exist for exactly that purpose: how we test sets out the method, how we make money names every commercial relationship, and corrections lists everything published here that turned out to be wrong.

That last one is the one worth reading first. Any site can claim to be careful. A site that publishes its own mistakes has to be.
