Home · Privacy policy

Last updated 3 August 2026.

Who we are

Feequity, operated by Jacob Shasha, operating as Feequity. Contact: hello@feequity.com.

What we collect

Your email address, only if you enter it to receive the portfolio tracker. Used to send you the link and occasional updates about the site. You can unsubscribe from any email and the address is deleted on request.

Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are read. This collects information such as approximate location, device type, referring site and pages viewed. It is not used to identify you personally.

Affiliate tracking. When you follow a partner link, the destination may set a cookie recording that you arrived from here, so that a commission can be attributed. That cookie is set by the partner, is governed by their privacy policy, and we do not receive your personal details from it.

We do not collect financial data. Nothing you enter into the spreadsheet is sent to us, because the file lives in your own account and we have no access to it.

Legal basis, for readers in the EU and UK

Consent for the email list, which you give by submitting the form and withdraw by unsubscribing. Legitimate interest for analytics, limited to understanding which content is useful.

How long we keep it

Email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. Analytics data according to the retention period set in Google Analytics, currently 14 months.

Who we share it with

Our email provider, for the purpose of sending the emails you asked for. Google, as the analytics provider. Nobody else. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, and we will act within 30 days. Email hello@feequity.com.

Cookies

Analytics cookies, and cookies set by partners when you follow their links. Browser settings can block both. The site works fine without them.

Children

Not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

Material changes will be noted here with a new date at the top.

Feequity publishes research about investing tools and brokers. It does not provide investment advice and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
