The short version

Yahoo Finance handles dividends without you doing anything, tracks cash and lots, and shows sector allocation for free. It is the most complete of the free options.

Google Finance is the fastest to set up since the June 2026 rebuild, because you can upload a broker CSV instead of typing. Portfolios are not fully on mobile yet.

Neither one totals what your broker charges you. That is not an oversight, it is simply not a number either company has any reason to put in front of you.

The gap, filled

Our free Google Sheets tracker does what both of them do, and adds the annual cost of holding the account. It lives in your own Drive and there is no signup wall.
