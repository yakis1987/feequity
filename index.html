How we work

Numbers carry dates

Every figure is checked against a first-party source and stamped with the day it was verified. Rates move, and a number without a date is a guess.

We publish corrections

When something turns out to be wrong we say so in the open rather than quietly editing it away.

We say who pays us

Some links can earn a commission. It never changes a ranking, and the pages that say a product is the wrong choice are the ones worth reading.

The number nobody calculates

Four trades a month at five dollars, plus a normal currency conversion charge, comes to roughly 377 dollars a year on a modest portfolio. Over a decade that is 3,770 dollars. Our free spreadsheet works out yours.
